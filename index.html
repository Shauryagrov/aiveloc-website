/ Our Approach

A studio built around the new craft of app-making.

AI accelerates the work. Humans set the direction, taste, and bar for quality.

01

AI-Powered Development

Modern coding agents handle the boilerplate, scaffolding, and iteration. The grunt work disappears so we can move at the speed of ideas.

02

Human in the Loop

Every release goes through human review — design, UX, accessibility, copy. AI ships drafts; we ship products people are happy to keep on their home screen.

03

Best of Both Worlds

AI velocity, human taste. The result: polished apps that ship in weeks, not quarters — without the rough edges that betray throw-it-together work.

/ About

A studio of one. Shipping deliberately.

Aiveloc is an independent iOS app studio founded and run by Shaurya Grover. He started the company in 8th grade. The bet: small teams using AI can punch above their weight — without losing the human taste that makes a product feel cared-for.

Every app on the shelf is shipped by hand — every screen, every interaction, every word, reviewed by a real human.
